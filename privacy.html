Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

Summary

Our calculators run in your browser — what you type into a tool is not sent to us. We only receive information you choose to submit through a form (e.g. report request, contact, contest entry, newsletter). We never sell personal information.

Information we collect

  • Form submissions: name, email and the details you provide (e.g. birth date for a report). Forms are delivered to us by a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit).
  • Usage data: if you accept analytics cookies, anonymised page-view data via Google Analytics.
  • Advertising: once enabled, Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads. If you choose “Essential only”, we request non-personalised ads.
  • Local preferences: theme and consent choices stored in your browser.

How we use it

To respond to you, prepare requested reports, run contests, send newsletters you opted into, improve the site and keep it secure.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent, at any time via our contact form (topic: Privacy request). Residents of Québec (Law 25), Canada (PIPEDA), the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA) have additional rights, which we honour.

Third parties

Hosting (GitHub Pages), forms (FormSubmit), fonts (Google Fonts), video (YouTube, loaded only when you press play, using youtube-nocookie.com), analytics and ads (Google, if enabled). Their own privacy policies apply.

Google advertising cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and contests are for adults only.

Retention & security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose collected and use reasonable safeguards. No internet transmission is 100% secure.

Changes

We will update this page when practices change.
