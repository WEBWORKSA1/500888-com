Hongbao etiquette in 60 seconds

  • Even, never 4. Even numbers for happy occasions; 8 and 6 are favourites, 9 for elders' longevity.
  • New notes. Fresh bills show respect and a fresh start.
  • Both hands. Give and receive with both hands; don't open it in front of the giver.
  • Weddings: cover at least the cost of your banquet seat.
  • Funerals: a white envelope with an odd amount — never red.

FAQ

Which amounts should I avoid?

Anything containing 4, and the amount 250 (slang for 'idiot'). Odd amounts are reserved for funerals.

Is it rude to give coins?

Yes — traditionally use crisp, new banknotes. Many people visit the bank before New Year to get fresh bills.

Who gives hongbao at New Year?

Married adults give to children and unmarried younger relatives; adults also give to their parents and grandparents, and employers to staff.

Can I send a digital hongbao?

Yes. WeChat Pay and Alipay red packets are hugely popular — the same number etiquette applies.
