FAQ

When does a Chinese zodiac year start?

On Lunar New Year, which falls between 21 January and 20 February. Someone born on 20 January 2023 is a Tiger (2022's sign), not a Rabbit.

What is my element?

Each year also carries one of five elements — Metal, Water, Wood, Fire, Earth — set by the last digit of the lunar year. 2027 is a Fire Goat year (丁未).

What does 'Ben Ming Nian' mean?

Your zodiac's own year (本命年) — e.g. Goats in 2027. Tradition says to take extra care and wear red for protection.

Which signs clash in 2027?

Traditionally the Goat (Ben Ming Nian), Ox (direct clash), Rat (harm) and Dog (punishment) are advised to be cautious in a Goat year.
