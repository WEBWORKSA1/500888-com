Frequently asked questions

What does 500888 mean in Chinese?

Read digit by digit it is wǔ-líng-líng-bā-bā-bā. 8 (bā) sounds like 发 fā, 'to prosper', so 888 is a classic triple-prosperity sequence. 500 evokes scale and achievement — Chinese media call the Fortune Global 500 '世界500强'. It is a folk, sound-based reading, not an official meaning.

Why is 8 lucky and 4 unlucky?

Chinese number symbolism is driven by homophones. 8 (bā) sounds like 'prosper' (fā), while 4 (sì) sounds like 'death' (sǐ). That is why many buildings skip the 4th floor and why prices often end in 8.
