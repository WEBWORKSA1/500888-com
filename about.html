About 500888

An independent, free resource on the culture of prosperity — numbers, zodiac, feng shui and festivals.

Our mission

To make the rich, playful and economically significant world of Chinese number culture accessible to everyone — families sending red envelopes, couples choosing a wedding date, travellers, students, and businesses serving Chinese-speaking customers worldwide.

What we believe

  • Culture, not superstition-selling. We explain meanings and their origins, flag folk vs. classical interpretations, and never promise outcomes.
  • Data where it exists. When we say lucky numbers carry a premium, we cite the auction, study or source.
  • Both Mandarin and Cantonese. Meanings differ by dialect; we say so.
  • Free tools, private by default. Calculators run in your browser.

Editorial standards

Guides are researched from academic papers, government data and reputable publications, and are reviewed for accuracy before publishing and when festivals or dates change. Spot an error? Tell us — we correct quickly and transparently.

Independence

500888.com is not affiliated with any company, lottery, bank or organisation that uses “500”, “888” or “500888”. See our trademark & copyright disclosure. We do not promote gambling.
