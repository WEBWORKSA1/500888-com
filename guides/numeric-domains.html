Why short number domains are a real asset class in China — and how to value one.

Why numbers beat words

For Chinese-speaking users, a short string of digits is easier to type and remember than an English word, and numbers carry meaning through sound. That makes numeric domains a distinct, actively traded asset class.

Record sales

In 2015 Qihoo paid US$17 million for 360.com, a record domain sale at the time. Industry commentary continues to note a price gap between Chinese and Western buyers for numeric names, with new demand from Chinese technology and AI companies.

What drives value

  • Length: shorter is exponentially rarer.
  • Digits: 8, 6 and 9 add value; 4 subtracts; 0 and 1 are neutral-to-positive.
  • Pattern: repeats (888), mirrors (8558), ascending (123) and meaningful phrases (168, 518, 520).
  • Extension: .com dominates; .cn and .net follow.

How to evaluate a numeric domain

  1. Score it with the Lucky Number Analyzer (use “Domain” mode).
  2. Check both Mandarin and Cantonese readings for unintended meanings.
  3. Search trademarks in the markets you'll operate in.
  4. Review comparable sales and liquidity in the same length/pattern class.
  5. Want a second opinion? Request an appraisal.
