The Fire Goat (丁未)

The Goat (also called Sheep or Ram) is the eighth animal of the zodiac, associated with gentleness, creativity and community. 2027 pairs it with yin Fire — warmth, expression and artistry. The previous Fire Goat year was 1967. People born in Goat years (…1991, 2003, 2015, 2027) enter their Ben Ming Nian, traditionally a year to wear red for protection.

Traditions

  • Spring cleaning before New Year's Eve — sweeping out bad luck (not after!).
  • Red decorations: couplets (春联), paper-cuts and the upside-down 福 (“fortune arrives”).
  • Reunion dinner with fish (鱼 yú = surplus), dumplings (shaped like ingots) and nian gao (higher every year).
  • Red envelopes for children, unmarried juniors and elders — use our Hongbao Calculator.
  • Fireworks to scare away the mythical beast Nian (where permitted).

Taboos

  • No sweeping or taking out rubbish on New Year's Day — you'd sweep away luck.
  • Avoid breaking things, arguing, crying or saying “death”, “four” or “poor”.
  • Don't wash or cut your hair on the first day.
  • Don't lend or borrow money — it sets the tone for the year.

Greetings

  • 新年快乐 Xīnnián kuàilè — Happy New Year
  • 恭喜发财 Gōngxǐ fācái — Wishing you prosperity (Cantonese: Gung hei fat choy)
  • 羊年大吉 Yáng nián dàjí — Great luck in the Year of the Goat
  • 三羊开泰 Sān yáng kāi tài — “Three goats bring prosperity”, a classic Goat-year blessing
