Why it works

Numbers are a signal your customers already read

Research published in the Journal of International Marketing found Chinese price advertisements favour the ending digit 8 and avoid 4. A study of ~117,000 Vancouver home sales found addresses ending in 8 sold at a 2.5% premium while those ending in 4 sold at a 2.2% discount — concentrated in neighbourhoods with many Chinese residents.

Getting this right costs almost nothing. Getting it wrong — a 4 in a gift price, a 250 bundle, a launch on Ghost Festival — can quietly cost sales.
