Quick wins for any home

  • Clear the entrance. Qi enters through the main door — keep it lit, clean and unobstructed.
  • Command position. Place desk and bed so you can see the door without being directly in line with it.
  • Balance the elements. Plants (wood), candles (fire), ceramics (earth), metal frames (metal), water features (water).
  • Fix what's broken. Leaky taps and stuck doors symbolise money and opportunity draining away.

FAQ

What is a Kua number?

A number from 1 to 9 (never 5) from the Eight Mansions (八宅) school of feng shui, calculated from your birth year and gender. It sorts people into the East or West group and gives four favourable and four unfavourable directions.

Why does my birth date matter if it's only the year?

The feng shui solar year begins at Li Chun (around 4 February). If you were born in January or before 4 February, the previous year is used.

How do I use my directions?

Face your Sheng Qi (wealth) direction when working, negotiating or studying; sleep with your head toward Tian Yi (health); place your front door or desk to support your best directions where practical.
